## Post

New AI releases are easy to collect. Knowing which ones deserve your attention is harder.

I’m building a free AI Model Release Monitor around three practical questions:

• What changed?
• What fits the tools I already use?
• What might lower the cost of getting a useful result?

The pilot brings together a release catalog, selected workflow-fit reviews, developer changes, documented version/access comparisons, and a cost-scenario calculator.

You can browse everything and filter it, or choose your providers for stack-relevance flags. An optional exact-product profile stays in page memory; the app does not upload it.

The distinction matters: “new” is not automatically “useful,” and cheaper tokens are not automatically cheaper work.

This is an early pilot, not an exhaustive or independently benchmarked recommendation engine. Unknowns stay visible, predecessor history is incomplete, and “Test now” means evaluate, not deploy.

Try it: https://ai-release-notes.pplx.app

What would make this genuinely useful in your workflow: better version history, clearer access limits, or more relevant change alerts?
